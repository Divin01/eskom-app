❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my report confidential?

Yes, all reports are treated confidentially and investigated thoroughly by our professional team.

How long does an investigation take?

Most investigations are completed within 2-4 weeks depending on the complexity and location.

Can I see investigation progress?

Yes, you can track your case status in real-time through your dashboard.

What happens if theft is confirmed?

Confirmed cases are escalated to the appropriate authorities for action and recovery.
